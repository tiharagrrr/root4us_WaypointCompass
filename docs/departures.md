# Departures from the design

Where a built screen deliberately differs from its Figma frame, and why. `/fidelity <frame>` adds a
row for every intended difference; anything else that differs is a bug.

| Screen | Designed | Built | Why |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A0 Sign in | "Forgot password?" link | "Forgot password? Ask your admin." as plain text | No password-reset route exists yet (open question in specs/identity/spec.md) |
| A0 Sign in | "Email or phone" signs in with either | "Email or username" with a password; a phone number opens D0a with the number filled in; links under the card lead to the driver and dock sign-ins | Drivers sign in with an SMS code (D0a, D0b), never a password; the frames give the other two sign-ins no entry point |
| D0a, D0b Sign in · driver | Two phone frames | One screen in two steps (number, then code) in the dock sign-in's layout, with "Send again" after 60 s and a link to the demo inbox | Built before a fidelity pass against 244:828 and 244:937; the layout follows L1m's card so the three sign-ins read alike |
| D12 Account, D13 Sign out | Account with language and notifications; a sign-out dialog | A profile card, the phone's id and a Sign out card that waits for the outbox to drain (no dialog) | Built so a driver has a way out at all; language and notification preferences and the dialog follow in ROO-62 |
| /demo/inbox | No frame | A plain list of the SMS and emails the demo would have sent, refreshed every 5 s | Demo tool only; it exists so a judge can read a driver's code without a phone |
| L1 Sign in | Wrong-PIN and not-a-dock-device states only | The not-a-dock-device message says how to register the tablet; a link under the keypad leads to the email sign-in | A fresh browser is never a dock device, so the screen has to say what to do about it |
