## Datathon note

The Datathon rules prohibit pre-trained models (except for synthetic data generation or pre-processing), proprietary API-based modelling or pre-processing, and low-code or automated end-to-end modelling tools. Any AI assistance on Datathon work was limited to _describe, e.g. code completion and documentation_, and no LLM was used to produce predictions.
